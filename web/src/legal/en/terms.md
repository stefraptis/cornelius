## 1. What the Service is

The Service lets surgeons and surgical teams upload recorded laparoscopic operations, receive automated analysis of surgical phases, instruments and scene events, review their cases, create and answer learning questions, annotate videos, and share material inside their team or hospital.

The Service is intended for retrospective review, education, training and research on recorded operations. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose, it does not support decisions about an individual patient's treatment, and it must never be used for real-time guidance during an operation. Automated outputs (phases, instruments, events, narratives) are estimates produced by statistical models and heuristics, can be wrong, and must be interpreted by a qualified person.
